🚀 I. OPC Incubators and Ecosystem Development

1.1 Xinjiang’s First OPC Innovation Center Unveiled

On June 23, the OPC Innovation Center of Urumqi High-Tech Industrial Development Zone (New Urban District) was inaugurated at the Xinjiang Tianshan Science and Technology Innovation Institute. This startup incubation platform is specifically designed for AI developers and independent software engineers, aiming to cultivate a batch of “small but excellent” AI technology innovation enterprises with extremely low cost and highly elastic service models.

💡 Key Significance: This indicates that the OPC model has evolved from a grassroots spontaneous behavior to a government-recognized startup incubation direction. Xinjiang, as the core area of the “Belt and Road” initiative, is taking the lead in layouting OPC innovation centers, which has demonstration significance.

1.2 Chongqing Nanan District Plans 20 OPC Offline Communities

On June 27, at the 2026 International Innovation, Entrepreneurship, and Creativity Ecosystem Conference and the launch event of the General Artificial Intelligence “Starry Sky” Challenge, Chongqing Nanan District announced that it will plan and build 20 offline communities at high standards, providing 3,000 exclusive workstations to offer low-cost, high-efficiency development environments for OPC entrepreneurs.

This initiative marks the transition of the OPC model from virtual space to the real economy, with offline communities becoming an important support for one-person company entrepreneurs.

💰 II. OPC Commercialization Paths and Success Cases

2.1 Chengdu Seede AI: Funding Breakthrough for the OPC Model

Long Yi, founder of Seede AI, is a practitioner of the domestic OPC (One Person Company) model. In mid-2024, he launched the Seede AI project as a one-person company, completing the entire process of product R&D and early-stage operations single-handedly.

Core Data:

  • Registered Users: Over 300,000
  • Paying Users: Over 10,000
  • First-Round Funding: $1 million

Seede AI relies on its proprietary rendering engine and visual description language, breaking away from the mainstream text-to-image technology route, solving industry pain points such as AI design text corruption, non-fine-editable finished products, and aesthetic convergence. It provides visual creation services such as PPTs, posters, and infographic posters for e-commerce, SMEs, and workplace personnel.

💡 Key Takeaway: A one-person company is not a “small workshop.” By focusing on vertical scenarios and solving real pain points, it can equally gain user recognition and capital favor.

2.2 Six Revenue Paths for OPC

According to industry practices, the main paths for one-person companies to make money with AI agents include:

  1. Custom AI Agent Development for SMEs

    Undertake enterprise projects such as customer service agents, sales assistants, content generation tools, and data processing workflows. Single project fees range from several thousand to tens of thousands of dollars.

  2. Developing and Selling Subscription-Based Universal AI Agent Products

    Develop AI agent products for vertical audiences (e.g., e-commerce customer service agents, content creation assistants for self-media), achieving passive income.

  3. Content Entrepreneurship Powered by AI Agents

    Automate topic selection, writing, editing, and publishing through AI agents to improve output efficiency and expand content scale.

  4. AI Skills Training and Consulting

    Transform practical experience into courses or consulting services, providing AI transformation guidance for traditional industry practitioners.

  5. AI Data Analysis and Decision Support Services

    Provide SMEs with professional services such as AI-based market analysis and user feedback interpretation.

  6. AI Tool Integration and Process Optimization

    Help enterprises integrate multiple AI tools and design automated workflows to improve operational efficiency.

🧠 III. Theoretical Breakthrough: The Proposal of OPE Concept

3.1 What is OPE?

Dr. Zhang Qi, President of Microsoft AI Asia Pacific, proposed the “OPE (One Person Enterprise)” concept in May 2026—under the deep empowerment of generative AI and agents, individuals are becoming the “minimum innovation system” capable of scheduling tools, systems, and global resources.

OPE Applies to Three Scenarios:

  1. New work methods and organizational forms within large companies
  2. Work method upgrades for teams with fewer than ten people
  3. True personal independent entrepreneurship

The core logic is: with the help of generative AI and agents, individuals can expand their capability boundaries, build virtual teams, complete the full-chain closed loop from creativity to product, and ultimately grow into an efficient and autonomous creation system.

3.2 AI Agents Become Core Productivity

In 2026, AI tools have leapfrogged from auxiliary roles to core productivity units that are programmable, collaborative, and capable of undertaking systematic tasks. By comprehensively utilizing AI super employees (AI Agents), automated tool chains, and cloud services, individual entrepreneurs can build and operate a micro-enterprise covering the entire business chain with unprecedented efficiency.

The essence of a one-person company is the great外延 of individual capabilities through technology leverage.

⚠️ IV. Risks and Reflections: Returning to the Essence of Business

4.1 AI Entrepreneurship Cannot Be Blind

Industry expert Frank gives two practical suggestions for AI entrepreneurship:

Suggestion 1: Build a Personal Traffic Foundation

Under the background of AI tool democratization, traffic will become the core asset of one-person companies. Establish personal branding and accumulate precise users by continuously outputting valuable content.

Suggestion 2: Return to the Essence of Business, Anchor on Real User Needs

Technology is no longer the core threshold, and super individuals are not fighting alone. Avoid using AI for the sake of using AI; start from users’ real needs and find scenarios where AI can create value.

4.2 The Importance of Industry Ecosystem

A one-person company is not “one person fighting”; the industry and community ecosystem can bring:

  • Peer Network: Exchange experiences and learn from each other with other independent entrepreneurs
  • Industry Resources: Opportunities such as client referrals and partner matchmaking
  • Implementation Collaboration: Find complementary collaborations in technology, design, operations, etc.

🔮 V. Trend Outlook: OPC Development Predictions for H2 2026

5.1 Technology Threshold Continues to Decline

The rapid development of domestic large models enables one-person companies to use high-quality AI capabilities at lower costs. The natural advantages in Chinese understanding and localized scenarios will help individual entrepreneurs gain more professional business insights.

Key Capability Upgrades:

  • Content Creation Efficiency: From article writing to visual design, from video editing to voice-over production, work that previously required multi-person collaboration is compressed to one person’s fingertips.
  • Customer Service Capability: Intelligent customer service systems help one-person companies achieve 7x24-hour customer response.
  • Data Analysis and Decision-Making: AI becomes the most reliable “strategist” for one-person companies.

5.2 Creativity and Execution Become Key

As technology thresholds continue to decline, creativity and execution will become the key factors distinguishing excellent one-person companies. AI will not replace entrepreneurs, but it is reshaping the definition of entrepreneurship.

One person, one computer, one set of AI tools—this is becoming the most potential startup configuration in 2026.

5.3 Three-Stage Evolution of the OPC Model

According to the “2026 Global Artificial Intelligence OPC Model Business Insight Report,” the OPC business model will upgrade along the following path:

  1. Project Customization Service Stage: Undertake client needs in a project-based manner, quickly validate business models.
  2. Productized Service Stage: Productize service capabilities to achieve standardized delivery and scalable replication.
  3. Resource Integration Platform Stage: Build a platform ecosystem, integrate upstream and downstream resources, and achieve exponential growth.
