1. Macro Trend: OPC Officially Enters Boom Phase

Record-breaking registrations: As of June 2025, China’s one-person limited liability companies have surpassed 16 million units. In H1 2025 alone, 2.86 million new registrations were added — a 47% YoY surge, representing 23.8% of all newly registered businesses. From Beijing Zhongguancun to Shanghai Lingang, Suzhou Industrial Park to Hangzhou, the “one-person army” model is sweeping across the country.

The World AI Conference (WAIC) in partnership with OpenCSG launched the world’s first dedicated incubation platform for super individuals and AI entrepreneurs — the “OPC Pioneer Challenge”. 5. Real-World Cases: How They’re Making Money with AI

📱 Case 1: Local Business Marketing Agent

A 28-year-old leveraged OpenClaw to build a marketing agent serving 52 local businesses. Within 3 months, monthly revenue hit ¥31,000 ($4,250) with costs of only ¥12,000 — a net profit of ¥19,000.

⚖️ Case 2: AI Contract Review System

Traditional industry entrepreneur Li Jiehui built an intelligent contract review system for law firms and a full-process electronic management system for enterprises. Tasks that required 3-4 people now done by one person, achieving 3-5x efficiency improvement and a 90%+ profit margin.

🛠️ Case 3: ¥5M Annual Revenue One-Person Company

The founder of opcwang.com shared: after running 17 AI Agents for a year, his one-person company achieved annual revenue of ¥5 million — proving that one person + AI can fully sustain scaled commercial operations.

💰 Three Main Profit Models

  1. Content Matrix (Traffic Farming) — AI mass-produces content distributed across multi-account networks; monetized through ad revenue
  2. Micro-SaaS (Mini Software Factory) — Identify pain point → AI rapid development → subscription billing. Find 1,000 paying users and you’re set
  3. Service Arbitrage (Middleman Model) — Take orders from traditional clients (translation, design, proposals), execute with AI, profit from the information gap

7. Risk Alert: Not All Glitters

⚠️ One-person company pitfalls to watch:

  • Content homogeneity — AI-generated content risk of platform penalties or demotion
  • Single point of failure — Solo founder bears all pressure with weak risk resistance
  • Data compliance — Privacy and security regulations can’t be ignored
  • Tool dependency — AI service price hikes or shutdowns have outsized impact
  • Recommendation: Don’t put all eggs in one basket; build backup toolchains
