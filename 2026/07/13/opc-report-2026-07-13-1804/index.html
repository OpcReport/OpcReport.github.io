🔥 The Big Story: Billion-Dollar OPC Prophecy Coming True

Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei’s 2025 prediction that “2026 will see the first billion-dollar one-person company” is rapidly becoming reality. AI tools have driven the cost of “doing” to the floor while elevating the value of “knowing what to do” to the ceiling. OPCs are no longer small workshops — they’re a new business species that can challenge traditional organizational structures.

The math is simple: AI tools have evolved from assistants to programmable, collaborative, and systematically capable core productivity units. A single individual can now accomplish full-chain business operations — from product design to market deployment — that once required entire teams.
