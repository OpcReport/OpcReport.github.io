🔥 The Big Story: Billion-Dollar OPC Prophecy Coming True

Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei’s 2025 prediction that “2026 will see the first billion-dollar one-person company” is rapidly becoming reality. AI tools have driven the cost of “doing” to the floor while elevating the value of “knowing what to do” to the ceiling.
