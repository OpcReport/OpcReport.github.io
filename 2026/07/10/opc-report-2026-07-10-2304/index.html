⚠️ Risks & Reminders (for founders)

  • Moat myth: Pure “use AI to produce content/code” is no longer a barrier — you must stack domain insight and delivery capability, or you’ll be drowned in homogenization.
  • Agent success rates are still limited: Upwork research shows Agent solo task success is low; human-AI collaboration success jumps. OPC is not “hand it to AI,” but “work in sync with Agents.”
