🔍 Industry Deep Dive

2026: The Year OPC Explodes

From Beijing Zhongguancun to Wuhan Optical Valley, from Shanghai Lingang to Shenzhen’s Model Power Camp, over 12 million individual entrepreneurs are flooding into the OPC (One Person Company) track. One person, one computer, one AI system—completing the entire business cycle from product development, content creation to market distribution.

Digital Employee Year: AI from Auxiliary to Dominant

2026 is called the “Digital Employee Year.” AI agents like “OpenClaw (龙虾)” have completely broken down traditional coding barriers. Tech “newbies” no longer need to learn programming languages—all they need is natural language conversations to transform vague requirements into operational business applications.

“OPC is essentially ‘One Person Company + AI Cluster’—individuals amplify capabilities through AI agents, achieving ‘one person, army-level’ results. One person + one set of AI agents = a company that doesn’t need commuting, stays emotionally stable, has explosive efficiency, and operates 24/7.”
— Professor Mao Ning, Nanjing University Business School
