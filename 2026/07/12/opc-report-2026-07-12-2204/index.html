5. Critical Thinking: Biggest Opportunity or Biggest Trap? {#challenge}

⚠️ Key Judgment: After reviewing hundreds of OPC cases, only three types are profitable. The majority face challenges like lack of core technical barriers, poor market segment selection, or inability to sustain customer acquisition.

5.1 Common Causes of OPC Failure

  • Lack of Core Competitiveness: Tools can be copied, but insight, execution, and industry accumulation cannot
  • Wrong Market Segment: Entering red ocean markets, unable to compete with established teams
  • Insufficient Customer Acquisition: Good products but can’t sell, revenue can’t cover operating costs
  • Time Investment Imbalance: Underestimating actual workload of “one-person company,” leading to burnout
  • Compliance Risks: Ignoring hidden costs of business registration, taxes, and legal compliance

5.2 OPC ≠ Going Solo

OPC is fundamentally not “one person doing everything” but “one person making decisions and creating, while AI and external collaboration networks handle execution.” Entrepreneurs still need:

  • Professional services for business registration, tax filing, legal compliance
  • Industry circles, network resources, project connection channels
  • Continuous learning and iteration capability
