Generic content tracks are saturated, but vertical domains still have plenty of white space. “AI helps small businesses with financial reporting” or “AI teaches elderly to do health exercises” — extreme verticalization is where the money is.

Large-scale adoption of AI PCs and AI phones drives privacy-focused apps and offline AI tools. Domestic LLMs are densely integrated into BYD, Mercedes-Benz and other new vehicles — smart cockpits become the first AI landing station.

Tencent Yuanqi, ByteDance Coze, Dify, FastGPT and other platforms are fully accessible. Gartner predicts over 80% of enterprises will use generative AI APIs by 2026. Ordinary people don’t need to code — just “drag and connect” with natural language to create super workflows.

TOP 8: AI Shifts from “Luxury” to “Daily Necessity”

Enterprise custom AI customer service systems dropped from 500K RMB in 2023 to 50K RMB in 2025, projected to fall below 10K RMB in 2026. “My 60-year-old accountant aunt can now use AI for reconciliation.” — AI adoption exceeds imagination.

TOP 9: AI Startup Talent War Intensifies

Numerous AI entrepreneurs complain about inability to recruit top AI engineers. Good talent is either at big tech with high salaries or founding their own startups. Solutions: recruit from customers, hackathons, untapped pools, or attract through remote work and equity incentives.

TOP 10: Human-AI Collaboration SOP Standardization Wave

The “human-AI collaboration SOP” demonstrated by Hangzhou e-commerce entrepreneur is becoming a replicable standard process. AI handles “must-do but unwanted” repetitive work; humans focus on “creativity + decision-making.”
